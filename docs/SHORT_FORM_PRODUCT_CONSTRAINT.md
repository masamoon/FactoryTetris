# Short-form product constraint

Adopted 20 September 2026 after [independent adversarial review](reviews/2026-09-20-short-form-constraint-adversary.md). This is a product-direction and communication gate, not evidence that the current prototype is enjoyable, balanced, viral or commercially viable.

## North star

The real experience of building and improving an automated factory must be intelligible and appealing in a silent, portrait 30-second clip. The clip should make three things understandable without narration:

1. what the player changed;
2. what the automated system did because of that change; and
3. what meaningful consequence, constraint or next decision followed.

Every design and gameplay decision must serve this direction at the portfolio level. Major directions and material gameplay changes receive the complete clip review below. Serving the direction does not mean every rule must be individually photogenic. A change may:

- create the hook;
- improve portrait-scale readability or action-to-payoff cadence;
- preserve the truth of the demonstrated simulation;
- support accessibility, recovery or confidence to experiment; or
- provide the agency, progression, ownership and replayability that make the appealing moment part of a durable game.

Reject changes that make a better clip by making ordinary play shallower, more repetitive, less truthful, harder to use on a phone or less meaningfully automated.

## Permitted capture modes

**Real time is preferred.** A clip may begin from a disclosed prepared save when it is explicitly presented as developed-factory footage.

**Abridged footage is permitted with disclosure.** Cuts, speed changes and compressed waits must be identified. An abridged edit may remove uneventful time, but it may not:

- fabricate or reorder cause and effect;
- hide required costs, setup actions or recurring interventions;
- imply production rates or travel speeds the simulation does not have;
- hide imminent exhaustion, blockage, failure or depletion when it changes the meaning of the result;
- turn a temporary backlog release into a claimed sustained improvement; or
- replace simulated production with scripted spectacle.

Retain an uncut real-time capture or deterministic replay as the verification witness for every approved abridged scenario.

## Truthful 30-second clip review

For each candidate direction or material gameplay change, document:

1. **Starting state.** State the exact playable starting state and whether it is onboarding, ordinary play or a prepared developed save.
2. **0–10 second hook.** Identify the player's visible action and the genuine automatic consequence it causes.
3. **10–30 second extension.** Show a consequential production-system choice, constraint, improvement or readable next decision—not only more particles or a larger number.
4. **Capture mode.** Mark the footage as real time or abridged and disclose every cut, speed change and compressed wait.
5. **Uncut witness.** Preserve a real-time replay or capture proving costs, event order, rates, buffers, backpressure, depletion and outcomes.
6. **Silent phone read.** At portrait phone size, viewers should be able to identify what changed, what the factory did because of it and what meaningful choice could follow.
7. **Representativeness.** Determine whether ordinary play naturally produces the shown moment. Label contrived or developed setups and never imply that they are the opening.
8. **Ordinary-play companion review.** Evaluate agency, dominant strategies, repetition, recovery, mobile interaction cost, progression and replayability outside the selected 30 seconds.

Verdicts are **PASS**, **REVISE** or **REJECT**. PASS requires a truthful, readable scenario and no identified degradation of ordinary play. A clip PASS approves only the communication hypothesis.

## Reference policy

Successful or viral mobile-game clips may be used as explicit references for portrait framing, silent legibility, visual hierarchy and action-to-payoff cadence. Their popularity is selection-biased evidence and does not establish game quality, retention, monetization or fit for Gridforge. Do not copy deceptive playable-ad conventions, false rates, unrelated spectacle or pressure mechanics merely because they perform well in an advertisement.

## Current drill application

The first application is the drill end-of-reach problem. Independent review rejected repeatable `+1/+2` reach purchases as the core answer and did not pass moving the entire factory-connected drill after every shaft. A narrower fixed-base sectional-drill fork is now implemented as a bounded experiment:

- the base and factory output remain fixed;
- the head advances in visible eight-cell sections and leaves occupied return rail;
- the player confirms one second section after the first shaft and returning loads are clear;
- a timed tender visibly carries the real one-part extension from base to head before drilling resumes;
- one global extension and two drill bases cap the experiment;
- construction resources, travel, buffers and backpressure remain real across the full rail; and
- a second closer drill remains a credible alternative to one indefinitely extended rig.

The alternative is a second drill costing one part plus two ore at the authored nearer rich face. From the same first-part state both branches have equal eventual net ore after construction, while the new drill has a shorter first return and independent base and the extension reuses the existing route with fewer actions. That authored comparison is balance scaffolding, not proof of a meaningful choice.

Its truthful clip scenario must show the player's choice, tender or fresh-base commitment, genuine excavation and real ore return. It may not hide the cost, installation travel, route work, longer return distance, intervention frequency or near-term cap. The companion ordinary-play test must cover both branches, hands-free production time, maintenance actions, repeated-layout risk and whether players make meaningful frontier choices.

This is implemented behavior with a **PASS for the bounded experiment only**, not a permanent progression selection. See [the drill end-stop adversarial review](reviews/2026-09-20-drill-endstop-adversary.md) and [the exact implementation review](reviews/2026-09-20-drill-extension-implementation-review.md).
