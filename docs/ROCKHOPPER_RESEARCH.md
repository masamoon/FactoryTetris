# Rockhopper: research (tech tree proposal, revision 4, 2026-10-01)

Status: **revision 4. Round 3 gave a scoped PASS for a prototype** behind an off-by-default switch, once conditions 1–4 are written in; this revision writes them in. Nothing here is implemented, and the PASS is not evidence of fun or balance. Rounds 1–3 are in [the review](reviews/2026-10-01-rockhopper-research-adversary.md). The user chose the full review process (AGENTS.md, "Gameplay decision review") for this feature on 2026-10-01. A PASS would authorize only a prototype behind a menu switch, off by default.

## Why

The user, 2026-09-30: "Should we also have a tech tree?" The project's answer that day:

- Yes, but small, and **paid in ore, not credits**. A tree of "+10 % income" nodes makes the game more incremental, which is the opposite of the user's logistics feedback ([ROCKHOPPER_LOGISTICS.md](ROCKHOPPER_LOGISTICS.md)): "it needs to lean more heavily into automation and logistics … the player needs to have more of a say in logistics decisions."
- It should **gate new machines and recipes only**. Rocks stay credit-bought, and upgrades stay as they are. Bend posts and crossings stay free, because they are basic routing tools.
- It comes after factories exist, because a tree with nothing to unlock is just a shop. The factories prototype is merged behind its switch ([ROCKHOPPER_SORTER.md](ROCKHOPPER_SORTER.md)).

The problem it addresses (simulation, not playtest): **the gaps between rocks are empty.** The greedy bot (`npm run bot:rockhopper`, 60 min, seeds 1–3) finishes T1 at 6:10–6:24, reaches T2 at 10:50–10:58 and T3 at 28:26–32:15. Its long stretches are waits for the next slot's credit price, and nothing asks the player to build or place anything specific in those waits.

**What it does not do (round 2).** Revision 2 also claimed the tree would get players to build the copper-to-crystal route. Round 2 refuted that: a copper + crystal node was met without any route, by ordinary T3-to-T2 chaining, and the route the bot built cut income by 19–26 %. That claim is withdrawn, and copper + crystal is no longer a node (TT4).

## Measured ore supply (simulation)

Round 1 measured what reaches the hub **by belt**, which is all a Lab on a belt can ever take (seed 1, factories off; hand-laser and crumble flights excluded): copper about 233 chunk-equivalents/min at 5–10 min, gold 53/min at 10–15 min, crystal **2/min** at 30–35 min and 44/min at 55–60 min. The best single line at each node's start carried 50–65 Cu/min and 15–48 Ice/min (copper + ice), and 23 / 59 / 78 Cr/min (seeds 1 / 2 / 3, after T3).

**Pre-existing finding, logged separately:** the bot's belts carry almost no crystal for many minutes after T3 opens, because it drills T3 rocks late and lightly. That starves the premium copper + crystal factory with or without a tree. Whether a player drills crystal sooner is a playtest question.

## The idea in one line

**To learn an alloy, bring its two ores to the Lab together.** Each node is one alloy recipe. The Lab counts a pair only when both ores pass it close together, so each node asks the player to find the line where both arrive often, and to put the Lab there. That is a **light placement decision** (round 2: the best belt paired 1.0–3.6× faster than the second, median about 1.6×), not a construction puzzle.

## The decisions it gives the player

- **Where the Lab goes, once per node.** The best line is the one where both ores arrive often, usually a raw line before its smelter. A line that carries only one of the two counts nothing, however busy it is. The ghost shows which of the two ores a belt carries, not its rate, so the player still reads the factory to choose.
- **Which recipe first.** Up to four recipes are available at T3. Learning one early lets factories make it early. Round 1 found the order had no visible effect on pacing.
- **What it is not.** It is not a routing puzzle: none of the five nodes needs a new belt. Its job is to give each gap between rocks a short, visible goal tied to the factory, and to introduce recipes one at a time instead of all at once.

## Pacing it gives (round 2, simulation, seeds 1–3)

Research activity falls at about 1–5 min (copper + ice; factories unlock at 3:53–5:26), 11–18 min (the two T2 recipes) and from T3 on (the two crystal recipes). **18–30 min has nothing to research**, because T2 carries only three ores. That gap is a property of the content, not of the tree; filling it would need a new recipe or machine, which this proposal doesn't add. The first recipe doesn't hold the bot back: it buys its first factory at 12:05–12:15 with research and 11:45–11:53 without.

## Clip scenario (for the prototype only)

- **0–10 s (developed save, disclosed, T2 open).** The Lab sits on a T1 copper line with "Ice + gold · 0 / 60". Its ice half-ring is lit; the gold half is dark, and nothing counts.
- **10–20 s.** The player taps the Lab, then Move, and drops it on a T2 drill belt (in the bot, ice + gold is learned on the T2 ice rock, which also carries gold). The ghost lights both chips as it crosses that belt. Within seconds an ice chunk and a gold chunk arc into the dome, and the count reads "1 / 60".
- **20–30 s (cut, disclosed with its sim time).** The count completes, "Ice + gold learned", and a factory already on that line, receiving ice and gold bars, makes its first ice-and-gold alloy with a pop. The witness logs the time from "learned" to that first alloy, and the cut discloses it.

The beat that matters (an action makes the counter start) happens within seconds and needs no cut. The uncut witness is kept and logs the time to completion and the alloy rate. The clip shows a placement, not a route, which is what the feature is.

## Evidence the prototype must produce

- **The bot (TT9), 60 min, seeds 1–3,** against the factories bot without research: each node's time; Lab moves per node; the first factory's time; T2, T3 and T4 times; income at 20, 30, 45 and 60 min; the value the Lab took; the share of copper bars reaching a crystal factory.
- **The crossings stress tool with research on:** 0 locks, 0 stalls.
- **The 390 px mock (TT8), reviewed before any code is written, and a screenshot after.**
- **Tests:** a pair needs both ores in the hold; one entry per ore; `LAB_HOLD` discards; alloys never taken; an emptied bundle disappears; the belt never waits; the anchor follows the belt and re-snaps after re-target, bend, splice, move and sell (heir); a plate pushes the anchor; factories gated in the command, the tray and the bot; unlearned recipes pass at once; copper + crystal pairs without research; grants on load (with and without `known`); a malformed field resets; the save round trip and an old v3 save loading with research off.

## Open questions

- Does skimming pairs read as "feeding research", or as a tax on a line?
- Are 2–5 minute goals worth having, or busywork between rocks, given that each is one Lab placement?
- Is introducing recipes one at a time clearer than all at once, as the prototype has them today?
- The tree does nothing for 18–30 min or for the stretch after about 45 min, where the bot stops buying and never reaches T4 (1.8 M). Nothing exists to gate there; padding with income nodes would contradict the reason for the tree.

## Rejected or deferred

- **Credit-priced nodes and "+% income" nodes:** rejected; they add incremental play, not logistics.
- **A Lab as a machine with its own output belt:** rejected before review; a new machine's output starts at tier 1 (F2b), so clamping it into a wide line would throttle that line, and moving it would either cost a widen or reintroduce tier inheritance.
- **A Lab that consumes everything it receives:** rejected; rock is 50–74 % of every line.
- **Research at the hub (deliveries count toward research):** rejected; it asks nothing of placement or routing.
- **Gating rocks, docks, smelters, junctions, bend posts or crossings:** rejected; those are the core loop and the basic routing tools.
- **Per-ore progress (revision 1):** rejected in round 1; a free drag between two belts did every two-ore node.
- **Gating belt tiers (revision 1's Wide belts):** cut in round 1.
- **A copper + crystal node (revision 2):** cut in round 2; it was met without a route and delayed the premium.
- **A pairs-per-minute readout on the ghost:** rejected; it would turn the one decision into a read-off. The ghost shows which ores a belt carries, not how fast.
- **The sorter:** still deferred ([ROCKHOPPER_SORTER.md](ROCKHOPPER_SORTER.md)). The Lab does not need it, because it skims.
