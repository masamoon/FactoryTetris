# Current design and validation

The active default runtime is the Asteroid Works prototype. The earlier ten-level tile workshop remains available at `?mode=tiles` with an independent save namespace.

The project-level product gate is now the [short-form product constraint](SHORT_FORM_PRODUCT_CONSTRAINT.md): every major direction and material gameplay change must support a truthful, silent, portrait 30-second demonstration of player-caused automation without sacrificing ordinary-play depth, mobile usability, recovery or simulation truth. Abridged edits require disclosure and an uncut witness. A clip PASS is communication evidence only.

Latest implementation, 2026-09-20: the user proposed asteroid mining that begins with manual extraction and grows into an incremental factory with meaningful planning. See [ASTEROID_AUTOMATION_DIRECTION.md](ASTEROID_AUTOMATION_DIRECTION.md) for the leading hypothesis. The first earned part now presents a bounded expansion fork: install one visible eight-cell extension on the fixed-base drill, or spend that part plus two ore on a second drill at a nearer rich face. Repeatable per-cell reach upgrades and whole-base relocation were not adopted. The exact fork received adversarial PASS for this experiment and deterministic/browser validation; no human balance or enjoyment conclusion follows.

The [full adversarial design review](ADVERSARIAL_DESIGN_REVIEW.md), begun 2026-09-19, reopens the product direction against a factory-first mobile brief with a visible 10–30 second payoff. It includes independent mechanics/product reviews, actual phone captures, and scoped prototype proposals. Proposed toy-factory, timing, routing, footprint and continuity experiments are not implemented or validated for enjoyment.

See [POC_PLAYTEST.md](POC_PLAYTEST.md) for exact mechanics, budgets, reference costs, independent adversarial findings and unresolved engagement questions. Historical design proposals and review scopes are preserved in [GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md](GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md); an earlier proposal is not evidence of the current implementation.

Validation uses the actual deterministic simulation and browser inputs. Winning replays prove solvability only. The demonstrated level-five rebuild sequence is not evidence of a successful reusable production strategy; blocked completed-product surplus remains an important human playtest concern.
