# Rockhopper: ore picks ("dig this ore first") (revision 2, 2026-10-04)

Status: **prototype built behind a menu switch, off by default, after one adversarial review round (REVISE).** The review's conditions are written in below, except one: no reach cap makes picks both pay and keep drill placement a decision (P1). On 2026-10-04 the user chose "whole rock" (an explicit override of that condition, for the playtest): the prototype ships with an unlimited reach, which the review says erases placement. Nothing here is playtest evidence.

## Why

The user, 2026-10-04: "should we have 'filter drills' that only drill certain minerals? So we can make actual factory supply lines".

A strict filter was measured first and rejected. Plain rock is 50 % (T4) to 74 % (T1) of every rock's cells, and a rock is replaced only once it is nearly spent. Drills that mine only their ore strip it and then stand idle: 97 % of the time on classic rocks, with income down from 135/s to 5/s. Even with an extra plain drill per rock to clear the stone, classic income fell 25–43 %. An ore pick ("this ore first, then anything") keeps every drill busy and leaves nothing behind.

## Rules

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| P1  | **A drill may have one picked ore** (`Drill.pick`: copper, ice, gold or crystal; never plain rock). While the rock has a cell of that ore within `PICK_REACH` rock radii of the drill's rim point, the drill digs the nearest one; otherwise it digs the nearest cell of anything, as today. `PICK_REACH` is `Infinity` in the prototype (see the review). A drill with no pick is unchanged.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| P2  | **The pick belongs to the drill.** It is kept across respawns (veins stay put per slot) and moves. A picked drill wears a dot of its ore's colour; the dot is **hollow** while none of that ore is within its reach (it digs anything meanwhile).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| P3  | **Free, any time.** Setting or clearing a pick costs nothing, like re-routing a belt. It takes effect at the next cell; a half-dug cell keeps its work.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| P4  | **UI.** With the switch on, the drill bubble gets a second row: "Dig first", then "Any" and one chip per ore the slot's rocks can carry (`TIER_ORE` plus the signature: 2 or 3), each with the ore's colour **and name**, at least 44 px tall. The picked chip is ringed (`aria-pressed`); tapping it again clears the pick. A chip for an ore the current rock no longer holds is dimmed. While a picked drill's bubble is open, the first cells it will dig are outlined, and a moved drill's ghost previews its pick too. Fits a 360 px screen with 4 chips (checked at slots 0, 3, 6 and 7). |
| P5  | **Switch.** Menu item "Ore picks: on/off", a setting kept across new games, **off** by default. Off, the sim ignores picks (drills dig as before) but keeps them, so turning it back on restores them. `State.orePicks`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| P6  | **Saves.** `pick` is optional on a saved drill; a value that isn't 2–5 is dropped (the drill loads with no pick) rather than refusing the save. `orePicks` loads as on only when saved as `true`. Stays in `rockhopper.save.v3`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| P7  | **Bot.** `npm run bot:rockhopper -- --slow --picks [--reach R]` turns factories and picks on: every T3/T4 drill picks crystal, and every T1 drill feeding a crystal factory picks copper. `tools/rockhopper-ore-picks.ts` is the layout witness (placement against picks, at a given reach).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## Evidence (simulation, 2026-10-04)

**Layout witness**, `npx tsx tools/rockhopper-ore-picks.ts --minutes 60 --reach R [--slow]`. Two drills on the first copper rock and two on the T3 crystal rock, through a smelter each into one crystal factory, classic field. "Fixed" drills sit at scripted rim spots, "best" at the spots with the most of their ore in their first 12 cells. Income is averaged from the start, at 60 min, seeds 1 / 2 / 3:

| Reach            | Rocks     | Plain fixed     | Pick fixed                                                    | Plain best      | Pick best                     |
| ---------------- | --------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Infinity         | slow-burn | 122 / 163 / 164 | 165 / 176 / 172                                               | 133 / 147 / 140 | 165 / 172 / 167               |
| 1 radius         | slow-burn | 122 / 163 / 164 | 118 / 195 / 163                                               | 133 / 147 / 140 | 126 / 192 / 132               |
| 0.6 radii        | slow-burn | 122 / 163 / 164 | 121 / 163 / 161                                               | 133 / 147 / 140 | 134 / 150 / 134               |
| any of the three | classic   | 138 / 184 / 157 | within ±3 % of plain fixed, except Infinity on seed 3 (+11 %) | 142 / 183 / 171 | −7 to +2 % against plain best |

- **Classic rocks: picks do almost nothing.** Where a drill stands matters more (+4 to +11 % for the best spot).
- **Slow-burn rocks, unlimited reach:** picks pay at 60 min (+5 to +35 % over plain fixed, +20 to +24 % over plain best), but pick fixed ≈ pick best, so **placement stops mattering for a picked drill**. Plain best still wins at 20 min (181 / 220 / 179 against pick fixed's 164 / 177 / 172).
- **A cap doesn't rescue both.** At 1 radius the result swings by seed (−3 to +20 % over plain fixed), and pick best loses to pick fixed on seed 3 (−19 %). At 0.6 picks do nothing.
- **Supply lines don't improve.** Copper + crystal alloys rose on some runs and fell on others (60 min, unlimited reach, slow-burn: 233→433, 646→611, 671→560). The gain is mostly raw crystal bars.

**Bot**, `npm run bot:rockhopper -- --minutes 60 --slow --seed N` with `--factories` (base), `--picks --reach 1`, `--picks --reach Infinity`. It is a scripted upper bound, not a player:

| Seed | Earned at 60 min (base → reach 1 → unlimited) | T4 reached            | Paired copper reaching crystal |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1    | 4.89 M → 5.10 M (+4 %) → 5.30 M (+8 %)        | 41:34 → 40:17 → 38:23 | 0 % in all three               |
| 2    | 5.15 M → 5.62 M (+9 %) → 5.87 M (+14 %)       | 38:41 → 37:30 → 35:14 | 2 % → 0 % → 0 %                |
| 3    | 5.56 M → 5.75 M (+4 %) → 6.16 M (+11 %)       | 38:06 → 36:19 → 34:12 | 0 % → 0 % → 1 %                |

Income spikes for the first 6–8 min after the picks start (T3 crystal comes in first), then drops below the base while those drills work through the stone the crystal left, and recovers when T4 opens. The bot still almost never sends copper to crystal, picks or not.

**Tests.** `tests/rockhopper-ore-picks.test.ts`: a picked drill digs only its ore while there is any, then anything; no pick (and a cleared pick) digs exactly as before; a reach cap limits the ore dug first; the switch off digs as if unpicked and keeps the pick; the dig preview follows the pick; bad picks are refused; picks survive a respawn and a save; malformed saved picks load as none. Browser (`tests/rockhopper.browser.spec.ts`, 360 px): off by default, the menu switch, tapping a chip sets the pick and tapping it again clears it, chips at least 44 px, and the switch survives a new game.

## Open questions

- Is picking the best ore on every T3/T4 drill a decision, or a chore? (Bot and review both say chore.)
- Does a player feel the hollow dot and dimmed chips as information, or as noise?
- If supply lines are the goal, the bot shows the missing step is routing copper to crystal at all, not what the drills dig. Ore picks don't change that.
