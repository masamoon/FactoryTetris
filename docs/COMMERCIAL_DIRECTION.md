# Commercial direction — hypothesis, not implementation

Reviewed 2026-09-19 in response to the user's question about a lucrative online/mobile structure. Independent reviewers: `commercial_structure_adversary` and `monetization_adversary`. No monetization, analytics SDK, accounts, or online services were added.

## Recommended product hypothesis

A portrait construction-puzzle campaign: short, pauseable factory commissions, clear pictured objectives, completion-driven chapter progression, and freedom of construction within each level. A simple optional album of completed factories can preserve a record of player investment. It does not grant power or introduce a separate decoration economy. Optional efficiency records compare only identical levels and rules; they do not gate progress.

Author the teaching chapters. Generate candidate layouts offline only when useful, then curate using real replays, recovery checks, strategy-transplant attacks, and human comprehension tests. Generated volume is not evidence of affordable, enjoyable content. A limited visual operation vocabulary aids understanding but the present two-operation PoC does not justify a long catalogue.

This direction passed as a commercial prototype. A progression map alone cannot make repeated solutions enjoyable. Level resets may still disappoint players who prefer a persistent factory; observe that rather than assuming an album solves it.

## Initial commercial test

**PASS as a hypothesis:** free introductory workshop, followed by a clearly scoped one-time paid unlock of a substantial authored level collection. Additional chapter packs can be tested later if they offer distinct challenges and content costs are sustainable. No lives, waiting penalties, paid retries, extra moves, hints, or permanent power advantages in this proposed model. These exclusions preserve a coherent puzzle product; they are not proven to maximize revenue.

**REVISE / defer:** cosmetic-only free-to-play and rewarded cosmetic ads. We have not established what players want to decorate, long-term attachment, repeated cosmetic demand, or enough acceptable ad opportunities. Do not assume these streams can finance free content.

**PASS for a later experiment:** an optional shared daily/weekly puzzle, after basic return motivation is established. Participation is untimed and carries no lost-streak penalty. Same level and rules allow comparison. Live PvP, clans, and a complicated service backend are deferred. No recurring content cadence is promised.

## Evidence needed

Observe unaided phone comprehension, voluntary progression into meaningfully different levels, delayed returns, reasons for retries, strategy diversity, and authoring cost. Then test actual purchase conversion at a disclosed price; purchase intent is weaker evidence than purchases. Track D1/D7/D30 return cohorts without treating a generic benchmark as a profit threshold. Paid acquisition only makes economic sense when observed net player revenue covers acquisition and attributable operating/content costs with adequate margin.

The initial commercial discussion used five teaching levels with known winning costs of 2/4/4/5/6 actions. Five further content experiments are now playable; see POC_PLAYTEST.md. No player retention, willingness to pay, advertising income, acquisition cost, or profit has been measured.

## External context consulted

- [GameAnalytics 2026 Mobile & PC Gaming Benchmarks](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-mobile-pc-gaming-benchmarks): 2025 calendar-year data from over 16,000 mobile games meeting a 1,000-MAU threshold; median mobile sessions approximately 3.1–3.5 minutes. No genre-level breakdown this cycle. These are sampled live games, not a prediction for this prototype.
- [AppsFlyer State of App Monetization 2026](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/app-marketing-monetization-report/): documents multiple ad/purchase/hybrid models and differing revenue/retention economics. Model adoption is not proof that a model causes profitability for Gridforge.

The paid-unlock recommendation is a judgement about fit and validation cost. It is not a claim that it has the largest possible revenue ceiling or will outperform free-to-play.
